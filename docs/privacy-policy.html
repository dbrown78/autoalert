Privacy Policy

ODIN AutoAlert  ·  Last updated: April 14, 2026  ·  Effective: April 14, 2026

AutoAlert collects only what is necessary to provide vehicle diagnostics, predictive maintenance, and mechanic discovery. We do not sell your personal data. We do not use your data for advertising.

1. Information We Collect

Account information. When you create an account: your name, email address, and a hashed password (plain-text passwords are never stored).

Vehicle and diagnostic data. Vehicle year, make, model, and VIN (if provided); OBD2 diagnostic trouble codes (DTCs); sensor readings (engine temperature, RPM, fuel trim, O2 sensor values, and other OBD2 PID values); scan history and timestamps.

Bluetooth data. AutoAlert connects to your OBD2 adapter via Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) to retrieve vehicle data. Bluetooth connection metadata (device name, connection state) is used only within your app session and is not transmitted to our servers.

Location data. Your approximate location is requested only when you use the Mechanic Finder feature, to find nearby repair shops via Google Places. Location is not stored on our servers and is only accessed while the app is in use.

Usage data. App version, device OS version, feature usage patterns, and anonymous crash reports to maintain the service.

2. How We Use Your Information

DataPurpose
Account infoAuthentication, account management, customer support
Vehicle & sensor dataDiagnostics, scan history, Foresight predictive maintenance alerts
LocationFinding nearby mechanics (one-time, per request)
Usage dataBug fixes and performance improvements

Vehicle sensor data powers Foresight, our predictive maintenance feature, which estimates component failure probability and remaining useful life. Analysis runs on our servers using machine learning models and results are surfaced only to you.

3. How We Store Your Information

Account and vehicle data is stored in a PostgreSQL database hosted on Railway (railway.app), a U.S.-based cloud provider. All data is transmitted over HTTPS (TLS 1.2+). Authentication tokens are stored in your device's iOS Keychain using Apple's Keychain Services — never in plain app storage.

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your personal data is deleted within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Data is shared only with Railway (as our hosting provider), when required by law, or to prevent fraud or protect user safety.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your data at any time. California residents have full CCPA rights (we do not sell data). EU/UK residents have full GDPR rights including portability and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

To exercise any right, email us at elitesellers201@gmail.com with "Privacy Request" in the subject line. We respond within 30 days.

8. Children's Privacy

AutoAlert is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. Contact us if you believe a child has provided their information and we will delete it promptly.

9. Security

We use HTTPS (TLS 1.2+) for all data transmission, bcrypt password hashing, JWT authentication with refresh token rotation, iOS Keychain for token storage, and rate limiting and input validation on all API endpoints.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. Material changes will be communicated via the app or email. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.
